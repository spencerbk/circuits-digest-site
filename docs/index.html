  • ST VL53LB is the smallest FlightSense ToF ranging sensor at just 4.0 × 1.8 × 1.0 mm
    cnx-software · 2026-11-24 10:48 UTC

    STMicroelectronics has just introduced the VL53LB (VL53LBCA), its smallest Time-of-Flight (ToF) ranging sensor, part of the FlightSense portfolio, with a size of just 4.0 × 1.8 × 1.0 mm. Like the earlier VL53L0X and VL53L1X models, it’s a single-zone ToF sensor featuring a 940 nm invisible vertical-cavity surface-emitting laser (VCSEL), but it offers a longer range of up to 8 meters and supports multi-object detection. It’s 30% smaller than the previous generation (VL53L3) and consumes just 50 µW in ULP mode, which makes it ideal for smartphones and space-constrained robotics and industrial systems.
