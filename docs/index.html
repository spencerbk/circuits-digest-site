  • Pi-Ener-lite – A compact 18650 UPS board for Raspberry Pi Zero with RTC and fuel gauge
    cnx-software · 2026-08-17 09:00 UTC

    Designed by ACE Design Studio in China, the Pi-Ener-lite is a dedicated UPS board made specifically for the Raspberry Pi Zero series. It features a standard flat-top 18650 lithium-ion battery holder and switches to battery power when external power is lost, while supporting simultaneous charging and discharging. The Pi-Ener-lite connects to the bottom of the Raspberry Pi Zero via seven spring-loaded pogo pins that handle both power delivery and I2C data communication, leaving the top GPIO header free for other HATs or expansions.   • Luckfox Lyra PLC – A Linux-based Rockchip RK3506B industrial controller with RS232, RS422, RS485, CAN bus, and relays
    cnx-software · 2026-08-17 05:10 UTC

    Luckfox Lyra PLC is a Linux-based programmable logic controller powered by a Rockchip RK3506B tri-core Arm Cortex-A7 SoC, equipped with dual Ethernet, WiFi 6 and Bluetooth 5.2 wireless connectivity, a 1.9-inch touchscreen LCD, and a range of I/Os. The I/Os are mostly exposed through two 11-pole terminal blocks with one RS232, one shared RS422/RS485, one CAN Bus, and two relays, as well as smaller connectors for I2C, GPIO, and debugging. The system can be powered via USB-C (5V only), terminal block pins supporting 7V-24V DC input, or an optional PoE module.
