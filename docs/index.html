  • Waveshare ESP32-S31-Zero – A tiny ESP32-S31 board with up to 32MB PSRAM, 32MB SPI flash
    cnx-software · 2026-11-07 03:00 UTC

    Waveshare ESP32-S31-Zero is a small ESP32-S31 development board that follows the form factor of the earlier Waveshare “Zero” boards, such as the ESP32-C6-Zero-B, RA4M1-Zero, and ESP32-S3-Zero, among others. The new tiny board still features a USB-C port for power and programming, as well as eighteen through and castellated holes for GPIO and power signals, but it boasts a more powerful ESP32-S31 dual-core RISC-V MCU clocked at up to 320 MHz, featuring 2.4 GHz WiFi 6, Bluetooth 5.3, and 802.15.4 connectivity, as well as up to 32MB on-chip PSRAM and a 16MB or 32MB SPI flash chip. Since the ESP32-S31 has quite a few extra I/Os, Waveshare also added a 40-pin high-density connector for LCD, touchscreen, SD card, USB 2.0, and extra GPIOs.   • 520 MHz STM32H7P4/STM32H7P5 Arm Cortex-M7 MCUs feature Hardware Signal Processor (HSP), optional Neural-ART NPU
    cnx-software · 2026-11-06 11:04 UTC

    STMicroelectronics has introduced the STM32H7P4 and STM32H7P5, two 520 MHz Arm Cortex-M7 MCUs part of STM32H7P family designed for digital power, motor control, industrial sensing, and edge AI applications. Both feature a Hardware Signal Processor (HSP) for accelerating mathematical and signal-processing operations, while the STM32H7P5 also includes an 85 GOPS Neural-ART NPU for Edge AI inference/TinyML. The MCUs feature up to 2MB dual-bank flash, 736KB SRAM, two high-resolution timers with up to 24 PWM outputs, three advanced motor-control timers, and four 12-bit ADCs. Other features include CORDIC and FMAC mathematical accelerators, three CAN FD interfaces, USB OTG Full-Speed, and support for external memory through Octo-SPI and FMC interfaces.
