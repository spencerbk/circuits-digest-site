  • 750 MHz GigaDevice GD32H7 Cortex-M7 MCU family gets up to 9.5MB Flash, EtherCAT for industrial applications
    cnx-software · 2026-11-17 07:53 UTC

    GigaDevice adds new industrial parts to the GD32H7 series of Cortex-M7 MCUs, running at up to 750 MHz. The new lineup includes the GD32H77R for robotic joint control, GD32H78D/77D for industrial HMI applications, GD32H78E/77E for real-time motion control, and GD32H789/779 for general industrial automation. The MCUs feature up to 9728KB of on-chip Flash, 1152KB SRAM including 640KB tightly coupled memory (TCM), and hardware accelerators for mathematical operations and digital signal processing. Depending on the variant, they also integrate a MIPI DSI display interface, an EtherCAT slave controller, industrial encoder interfaces, dual Ethernet, and three CAN-FD controllers. The devices target servo drives, humanoid robots, industrial displays, CNC machines, PLCs, and digital power systems.
