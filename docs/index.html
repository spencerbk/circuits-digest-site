  • Web-based breadboard simulator lets you build circuits, fix errors, and monitor designs with a built-in oscilloscope
    cnx-software · 2026-11-18 08:04 UTC

    Dev Kapadia’s de:volt is a breadboard simulator that runs in your browser. You can place parts and wires on a breadboard, and the circuit runs live the whole time as you build it. The project aims to be as close as a real breadboard would be. It reports errors and warnings, and you can monitor pins with its built-in oscilloscope and/or multimeter. For instance, an LED with no resistor burns out, a weak battery sags under load, a motor pulls current, and a 555 on the 4-channel scope shows its timing capacitor charging and discharging. de:volt highlights: Build circuits on a virtual breadboard using the 105 parts available from the interface: resistors, capacitors, diodes, transistors, LEDs, 555 timers, 74-series and CD4000 logic, EEPROMs, motor drivers, sensors, and more.
